# Private Client Agreement Notes — Service Availability and Liability

Do not publish this page on the public website.

This text is for the private agreement between the developer/template owner and the client/store owner.

## Service Availability and Limitation of Liability

The website is provided and maintained on a reasonable-efforts basis. The Developer does not guarantee uninterrupted service, error-free operation, continuous order availability, or specific business results. The Client understands that the website depends on third-party services and internet infrastructure that may experience downtime, delays, limits, price changes, account restrictions, or failures.

The Developer will make reasonable efforts to investigate and fix reported technical issues within the agreed maintenance scope. However, to the maximum extent allowed by applicable law, the Developer will not be liable for lost sales, lost profit, lost business opportunity, customer complaints, reputational harm, business interruption, or indirect/consequential damages caused by bugs, downtime, third-party services, internet issues, email delivery problems, hosting issues, or other technical failures.

The Developer’s total liability for any claim related to the website, services, maintenance, support, bugs, downtime, or data issues will not exceed the total service fees actually paid by the Client to the Developer during the three months immediately before the issue occurred.

For clarity, this cap is based on the actual monthly service fees paid during that period, even if the monthly price increased or decreased during those three months. Third-party costs, domain fees, hosting fees, database fees, email provider fees, Cloudinary fees, Redis fees, taxes, and other pass-through costs are not included when calculating the liability cap, unless they are included inside a single monthly service fee paid directly to the Developer.

This limitation does not apply where liability cannot legally be limited or excluded under applicable law.

Legal review recommended before use with larger clients, high-value projects, online payments, sensitive data, or business-critical stores.
